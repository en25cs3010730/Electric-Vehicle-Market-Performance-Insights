# ⚡ EV Market Analytics

### Electric Vehicle Market Analysis using Tableau and Flask

## 📌 Project Overview

EV Market Analytics is an individual data analytics project focused on exploring the Electric Vehicle (EV) market through vehicle specifications, manufacturer details, market performance, battery technology, pricing, customer ratings, and environmental impact.

The project uses an EV dataset containing 37 attributes to analyse different aspects of electric vehicles. Interactive visualisations are created using Tableau Public and integrated into a Flask-based web application to present insights in an accessible and user-friendly format.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse the electric vehicle market using data-driven techniques.
- Compare EV manufacturers and vehicle models.
- Explore battery capacity, driving range, and charging performance.
- Understand EV pricing and annual market sales.
- Examine customer ratings and vehicle safety.
- Analyse the environmental benefits of electric vehicles.
- Present analytical insights through interactive Tableau dashboards and stories.

## ❓ Problem Statement

The electric vehicle market contains a wide range of manufacturers, vehicle models, technical specifications, prices, and performance characteristics. Understanding and comparing this information can be challenging when data is presented in raw tabular form.

This project aims to organise and visualise EV market data through interactive dashboards and a web-based platform, helping users explore market information and compare electric vehicles more effectively.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The project uses an Electric Vehicle dataset containing 37 columns.

The dataset includes:

- Vehicle identification and model information
- Manufacturer and manufacturing country
- Model year and market region
- Vehicle category
- Battery capacity and battery type
- Battery life and degradation
- Driving range and energy efficiency
- Motor power and vehicle performance
- Charging capacity and charging time
- Vehicle price and insurance cost
- Warranty and resale value
- Safety ratings and autonomous driving level
- Annual sales and customer ratings
- Environmental impact and recommended use cases
