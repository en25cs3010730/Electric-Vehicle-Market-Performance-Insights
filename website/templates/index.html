ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding the EV Market

This project focuses on analyzing the electric vehicle market using data visualization. It provides insights into electric vehicle adoption, market growth, geographical distribution, and industry trends.

Interactive Tableau dashboards and stories help users understand the data and explore important market patterns.
